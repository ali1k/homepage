# You’re the voice I always hear  
  
My heart is home, it’s yours to stay,    
Come inside, don’t turn away,    
Every breath I give to you,    
This whole soul belongs to you.    
  
In my eyes, you always shine,    
In my world, your light’s the sign,    
Every thought, every view,    
Leads me back, back to you.    
  
You don’t need to search afar,    
You already know you are,    
In my heart, deep and true,    
There is only place for you.    
  
My heart is home, it’s yours to stay,    
Come inside, don’t turn away,    
Every breath I give to you,    
This whole soul belongs to you.    
  
Even when I feel I fall,    
You are there through it all,    
In my weakness, in my pain,    
You are strength I still contain.    
  
Stay, stay, don’t be far,    
You’re the light of who we are,    
Call, call, I am near,    
You’re the voice I always hear.    
  
In my heart… you remain…    
Through the loss… through the pain…    
  
My heart is home, it’s yours to stay,    
Come inside, don’t turn away,    
Every breath I give to you,    
This whole soul belongs to you.    
  
You are here… you are true…    
All I am… lives in you…
