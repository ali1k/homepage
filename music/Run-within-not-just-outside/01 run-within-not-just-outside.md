# Run within, not just outside  
  
(Inspired by Golshan-e Raz — unity, illusion, inner truth)    
   
In the garden, find the light,    
Hidden deep beyond the sight,    
Run within, not just outside,    
There’s a truth you cannot hide.    
  
All you see is just a veil,    
Every form is just a trail,    
What is near feels far away,    
Till you turn and look within.    
  
Drop the name, drop the face,    
There’s a deeper, silent place,    
In the flow, in the run,    
You and all become just one.    
   
In the garden, find the light,    
Hidden deep beyond the sight,    
Run within, not just outside,    
There’s a truth you cannot hide.    
  
Every step dissolves the line,    
Yours and mine begin to shine,    
In the breath, in the ground,    
There’s no lost and there’s no found.    
  
One, one, all is one,    
Moon and shadow, star and sun,    
Run, run, feel the same,    
All returns to one true flame.    
  
No divide… no apart…    
Just one light… one heart…    
  
In the garden, find the light,    
Hidden deep beyond the sight,    
Run within, not just outside,    
There’s a truth you cannot hide.    
  
All is one… all is light…    
Hidden deep… out of sight…
