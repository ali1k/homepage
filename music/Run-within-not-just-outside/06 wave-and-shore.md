# Wave and Shore  
  
(Inspired by “Wave and Shore” — motion = existence)    
  
I’m a wave, I’m made to move,    
In the motion, I find truth,    
If I stop, I fade away,    
I am living when I sway.    
  
You’re the shore, you stand so still,    
Calm and quiet, strong in will,    
But I burn, I rise, I fall,    
I can’t rest—I feel it all.    
  
Every crash, every climb,    
Is the rhythm, is the sign,    
I don’t break—I become,    
Every time I come undone.    
   
I’m a wave, I’m made to move,    
In the motion, I find truth,    
If I stop, I fade away,    
I am living when I sway.    
  
Stillness feels too close to end,    
I was born to bend and bend,    
Forward pull I can’t ignore,    
I am less when I’m at shore.    
   
Move, move, stay alive,    
In the motion, I survive,    
Run, run, feel the flow,    
That’s the only way I know.    
  
If I stop… I disappear…    
In the rush… I become clear…    
  
I’m a wave, I’m made to move,    
In the motion, I find truth,    
If I stop, I fade away,    
I am living when I sway.    
  
Rise and fall, rise and fall,    
That’s the meaning of it all…    
Move and breathe, don’t delay,    
Life is motion—ride the wave.
