# Maybe light will find my way  
  
(Inspired by Hafiz — hope, chance, light arriving)    
  
Maybe light will find my way,    
Maybe night will turn to day,    
Step by step, I still believe,    
What I seek, I will receive.    
  
If you pass this road I’m on,    
Even briefly, even once,    
All my world would start to shine,    
Every breath would turn divine.    
  
Like a spark upon the sea,    
One small sign could set me free,    
In a glance, in a ray,    
All my darkness fades away.    
  
Maybe light will find my way,    
Maybe night will turn to day,    
Step by step, I still believe,    
What I seek, I will receive.    
  
If the moon begins to rise,    
From the edge of hidden skies,    
Maybe just a single beam,    
Reaches into all I dream.    
  
Hope, hope, don’t let go,    
You don’t know what’s yet to show,    
Run, run, stay in line,    
Something good will come in time.    
  
Don’t give up… don’t turn away…    
Light can come… any day…    
  
   
Maybe light will find my way,    
Maybe night will turn to day,    
Step by step, I still believe,    
What I seek, I will receive.    
  
It may come… it may be…    
What I seek… comes to me…
