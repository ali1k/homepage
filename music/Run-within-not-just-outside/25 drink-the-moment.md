# Drink the moment  
  
(Inspired by Khayyam rubāʿī — short, rhythmic, repeatable)    
  
Run while you can, don’t wait, don’t wait,    
This fleeting life won’t hesitate,    
Drink the moment, don’t be late,    
Run your path, create your fate.    
  
Step by step, the road is yours,    
Time won’t knock on closed doors,    
What is gone won’t come again,    
So feel the sun, forget the pain.    
  
Run while you can, don’t wait, don’t wait,    
This fleeting life won’t hesitate,    
Drink the moment, don’t be late,    
Run your path, create your fate.    
  
Dust to dust, we rise and fall,    
Still we dance, we give it all,    
In this breath, in this beat,    
Life is now beneath your feet.    
  
Now, now, don’t delay,    
Night will turn into the day,    
Fast, fast, feel the flame,    
All of time is just a game.    
  
Run while you can, don’t wait, don’t wait,    
This fleeting life won’t hesitate,    
Drink the moment, don’t be late,    
Run your path, create your fate.
