# Let the love inside you grow  
  
Let the love inside you grow,    
Like a river soft and slow,    
In your heart, let it flow,    
That’s the only way to know.    
  
Every fear begins to fade,    
When a little love is made,    
Every wall you built so high,    
Starts to fall, you wonder why.    
  
In the giving, you receive,    
More than what you could believe,    
In the light that you share,    
There is meaning everywhere.    
  
Let the love inside you grow,    
Like a river soft and slow,    
In your heart, let it flow,    
That’s the only way to know.    
  
Love, love, let it rise,    
Like the dawn in open skies,    
Give, give, don’t hold tight,    
You will find your inner light.    
  
Let the love inside you grow,    
Like a river soft and slow,    
In your heart, let it flow,    
That’s the only way to know.
