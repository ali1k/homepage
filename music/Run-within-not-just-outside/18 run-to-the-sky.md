# Run to the Sky  
  
I wake beneath the endless sky,  
The sun is bright, the clouds float by.  
My heart beats fast, I feel the fire,  
The world is love, my soul’s desire.  
  
Rain falls down, it sings to me,  
Whispers of eternity.  
  
I run, run, run, I fly, I rise,  
I run, run, run, into the skies.  
Love is wind, the light is near,  
I run, I run, I have no fear.  
  
The sky is wide, my spirit soars,  
I run through fields, through open doors.  
The sun, the rain, the air, the song,  
I run, I run, I’ve found my home.  
  
Rain falls down, it sings to me,  
Whispers of eternity.  
  
I run, run, run, I fly, I rise,  
I run, run, run, into the skies.  
Love is wind, the light is near,  
I run, I run, I have no fear.  
  
No end, no edge, no need to hide,  
The sky is here, the love inside.  
Every step and every breath,  
I run, I run, beyond all death.  
  
I run, run, run, I fly, I rise,  
I run, run, run, into the skies.  
Love is wind, the light is near,  
I run, I run, I have no fear.  
  
I run, I run, I touch the sun,  
The sky, the rain, the love—are one.
