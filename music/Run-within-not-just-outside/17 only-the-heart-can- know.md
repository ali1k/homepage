# Only the heart can know  
  
I woke beneath a painted sky  
Where silence learned to sing  
The breath of life inside my chest    
Felt like a hidden wing  
I walked through echoes of my past  
Through shadows left behind  
And every step became a door    
To something more divine  
   
In every drop of falling rain  
A thousand secrets flow  
Whispers of a deeper love    
Only the heart can know  
  
   
I run through light, I run through time  
Through joy that feels like fire  
The sun is dancing in my veins  
Lifting me higher and higher  
I fly beyond what I can see  
Where love and sky are one  
In every breath, eternity  
I rise, I fall, I run
