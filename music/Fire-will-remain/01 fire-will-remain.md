# Fire will remain  
  
Ancient land, eternal flame,  
time won’t break your sacred name,  
hearts of iron, voices strong—  
we have carried you so long.  
  
Shadows fall, but we don’t bend,  
through the dark, we stand again,  
hand in hand, we guard the light—  
we were born to face the night.  
  
⸻  
Stand and fight, we don’t break,  
for the dawn we will not shake,  
through the dark, through the pain—  
fire will remain, fire will remain.  
  
⸻  
  
Children of Minab still say,  
blood was never meant to fade,  
every drop, a rising flame—  
calling out your ancient name.  
  
Lost or found, near or far,  
we remember who we are,  
night is deep—but it will end—  
light will live again, again.  
  
⸻  
  
Stand and fight, we don’t break,  
for the dawn we will not shake,  
through the dark, through the pain—  
fire will remain, fire will remain.
