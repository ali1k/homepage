# It is not fair  
  
You were kind, you helped a lot,  
You gave all the love you got,  
You would run, you rode your bike,  
Full of joy, full of life.  
  
You had dreams, you had a plan,  
You were more than just one man,  
With your wife and children near,  
You made home feel safe and clear.  
  
Then one night it all went wrong,  
War came fast, it came so strong,  
Not your fault, not your fight,  
Still it took your life that night.  
  
It is not fair.  
It is not fair.  
  
I still see your smile bright,  
I still feel your hopeful light,  
All your steps, your moving way,  
Live inside my mind each day.  
  
You helped people, gave your time,  
Now I miss you all the time,  
World feels worse without you here,  
Full of loss and full of fear.  
  
It is not fair.  
It is not fair.  
  
But your love is still around,  
In each small and quiet sound,  
In our hearts you will stay,  
You are not just gone away.
