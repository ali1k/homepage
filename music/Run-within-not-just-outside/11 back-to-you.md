# Back to you  
  
One look from you, I’m caught inside,    
Every road is closed aside,    
All I see and all I do,    
Leads me back, back to you.    
  
Something in you pulls me near,    
Soft and strong, so bright, so clear,    
I can’t turn and walk away,    
You are with me every day.    
  
You appear, then you’re gone,    
Still I keep holding on,    
In that glimpse, I lose control,    
You have taken all my soul.    
  
One look from you, I’m caught inside,    
Every road is closed aside,    
All I see and all I do,    
Leads me back, back to you.    
  
All the colors, all the light,    
Shine because you’re in my sight,    
Even when I’m far from you,    
Everything feels close and true.    
  
Stay, stay, don’t let go,    
You’re the only truth I know,    
Call, call, I will hear,    
Even when you’re not so near.    
  
You are near… you are far…    
Still you are… who you are…    
  
One look from you, I’m caught inside,    
Every road is closed aside,    
All I see and all I do,    
Leads me back, back to you.    
  
Without love… there’s no way…    
Only you… lead the way…
