# Lift me up, take me higher  
  
(Inspired by Hafiz — joy, presence, “bring the wine” energy)    
  
Bring the light, bring the fire,    
Lift me up, take me higher,    
Laugh out loud, feel the day,    
Let the dark just fade away.    
  
In this breath, in this now,    
Don’t ask when or don’t ask how,    
What is gone, let it go,    
Feel the joy begin to grow.    
  
Drop the weight, lose the fear,    
All you need is already here,    
Step by step, feel it rise,    
See the world with open eyes.    
  
Bring the light, bring the fire,    
Lift me up, take me higher,    
Laugh out loud, feel the day,    
Let the dark just fade away.    
  
Every road, every turn,    
Is a chance to live and learn,    
In the fall, in the climb,    
There’s a rhythm, there is time.    
  
Now, now, feel alive,    
In this moment, we arrive,    
Run, run, heart on fire,    
Lift it up, take it higher.    
  
Bring the light, bring the fire,    
Lift me up, take me higher,    
Laugh out loud, feel the day,    
Let the dark just fade away.
