# An ocean in an ocean in a drop  
  
A drop—awake, alone,  
Far from the endless known,  
Dust on a restless land,  
No sea, no guiding hand.  
  
Step—over stone and dry,  
Step—under open sky,  
A question in every stride:  
Where does the water hide?  
⸻  
Run—run—through the unknown,  
Small—but not alone,  
Run—run—feel the motion,  
Every step—toward the ocean.  
⸻  
A stream says, “Flow with me,”  
A river says, “Be free,”  
I follow every sign,  
But none of them are mine.  
  
I fall, I break, I bend,  
I lose the start and end,  
The farther that I roam,  
The less I feel at home.  
⸻  
Run—run—through the unknown,  
Small—but not alone,  
Run—run—feel the motion,  
Every step—toward the ocean.  
⸻  
The sound grows deep and wide,  
But echoes from inside,  
No shore appears in sight—  
Just something vast… and bright.  
  
Step—then the chase is gone,  
Step—then it all turns one,  
No distance left to cross,  
No seeker, no more loss.  
⸻  
A drop—no more apart,  
An ocean in the heart,  
What I was running to…  
Was always running through.  
  
Run—run—no more division,  
Drop and sea—one vision,  
Run—run—endless motion,  
I’m the drop—and I’m the ocean.  
⸻  
I’m the drop… I’m the ocean…  
Still in motion… still in motion…
