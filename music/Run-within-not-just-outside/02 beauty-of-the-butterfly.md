Beauty of the Butterfly   
  
A butterfly moves in the light,  
Wings of color, clear and bright,  
It never sees the shades it brings,  
The quiet fire upon its wings.  
  
It does not turn to look behind,  
It does not measure, weigh, or find,  
It only moves the way it must,  
With open air, with simple trust.  
⸻  
No need to reach, no need to prove,  
There’s something in the way you move,  
No need to search beyond your view,  
The light is already in you.  
⸻  
You don’t see what others see,  
There’s more to you than you believe,  
You look ahead, you look for truth,  
While something bright is living through you.  
  
Let it rise, don’t hold it down,  
You don’t have to earn that crown,  
You are enough, you always knew,  
The light you seek is shining through.  
⸻  
It moves across the fields of green,  
Through every space it’s never seen,  
It touches life, then passes by,  
Still unaware of every eye.  
  
No voice to tell it what it is,  
No mirror showing what it gives,  
And still it leaves a trace of grace,  
In every heart, in every place.  
⸻  
You don’t see what others see,  
There’s more to you than you believe,  
You look ahead, you look for truth,  
While something bright is living through you.  
  
Let it rise, don’t hold it down,  
You don’t have to earn that crown,  
You are enough, you always knew,  
The light you seek is shining through.  
⸻  
We search for more, we try to find,  
A better place, a different kind,  
We chase a shape we’ve never known,  
And miss the light we already own.  
  
Like wings that never turn to see…  
They are what they were meant to be.  
⸻  
You don’t see what others see,  
There’s more to you than you believe,  
No need to search, no need to prove,  
Your beauty lives in how you move.  
  
Let it rise, let it come through,  
There is nothing you must do,  
You are the light, steady and true,  
Not what you seek—but shining through.  
⸻  
A butterfly in open blue…  
It never knew… it always knew…
