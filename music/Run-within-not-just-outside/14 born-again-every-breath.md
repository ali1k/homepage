# Born again, every breath  
  
(Inspired by “هر دم تو را تولدی دیگر است” — rebirth & becoming)    
   
Born again, every breath,    
Life begins in every step,    
New each time, don’t resist,    
In the change, you exist.    
    
Not the past, not the pain,    
Not the loss you try to name,    
Every moment breaks the chain,    
You are never just the same.    
  
Let it go, let it fall,    
You don’t need to keep it all,    
In the shift, in the flow,    
There’s a self you start to know.    
  
Born again, every breath,    
Life begins in every step,    
New each time, don’t resist,    
In the change, you exist.    
   
Every end becomes a start,    
Every break reshapes your heart,    
In the dark, in the flame,    
You are rising once again.    
   
Again, again, rise anew,    
There is more inside of you,    
Now, now, feel the flame,    
You will never be the same.    
   
Every breath… something new…    
Every step… breaks you through…    
   
Born again, every breath,    
Life begins in every step,    
New each time, don’t resist,    
In the change, you exist.    
   
Again I rise… again I start…    
New each breath… a brand new heart…
