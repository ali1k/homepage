# Color the world one step at a time  
  
Step by step, I hit the ground,  
Every beat, a rising sound,  
Every step, a spark, a sign,  
I paint the world one step at a time.  
  
Step—blue rivers start to run,  
Step—cool waves catch the sun,  
Step—flowing in their line,  
I paint the flow one step at a time.  
  
Step—green fields wake and grow,  
Step—fresh leaves start to glow,  
Step—life begins to climb,  
I paint the earth one step at a time.  
  
One step, one spark, one living line,  
I color the world one step at a time,  
Blue keeps flowing, green will rise,  
Life keeps growing as I stride.  
  
Step—blue sky opens wide,  
Step—clouds drift as I glide,  
Step—endless and divine,  
I paint the sky one step at a time.  
  
Step—green trees breathe me in,  
Step—shade cools down my skin,  
Step—roots and roads align,  
I paint the path one step at a time.  
  
Step—blue again, the rivers call,  
Step—hear the water fall,  
Step—rhythm in my stride,  
I run along the tide.  
  
One step, one spark, one living line,  
I color the world one step at a time,  
Blue keeps flowing, green will rise,  
Life keeps growing as I stride.  
  
Step—orange sun lights my way,  
Step—burning into day,  
Step—fire in my climb,  
I paint the light one step at a time.  
  
Step—green returns once more,  
Step—stronger than before,  
Step—spreading far and wide,  
Growing with my stride.  
  
Step—blue fades into night,  
Step—stars begin to shine,  
Step—I still feel alive,  
I paint the dark with light.  
  
One step, one spark, one living line,  
I color the world one step at a time,  
Blue keeps flowing, green will rise,  
I build this world with every stride.
